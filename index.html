Who We Are

Safety is a Reclamation of Dignity

The Safety and Leadership Alliance is a nonprofit born from a conviction that self-defense is not just a physical skill. It is a reclamation of dignity, agency, and hope. We arrive not to deliver a program and leave, but to ignite lasting, sustainable change in the world's most vulnerable communities.

Declaration of Intent

We believe that every woman and girl deserves the tools to protect herself, the confidence to lead, and a community that sustains those capabilities without outside help.

We do this by training and certifying local women as coaches, ensuring that our work continues long after the instructors have gone home.

How It Started

A Refugee Camp. Hundreds of Survivors. A Mission Was Born.


In the summer of 2018, a small team of self-defense instructors traveled to a refugee camp in Northern Iraq — home to 15,000 Yazidi internally displaced persons — to teach self-defense to women and girls who had survived unimaginable atrocities at the hands of ISIS. On the first day, 20 students showed up. By the third day, 90.

"I feel powerful. The main reason why I want to learn is for the future. If someone wants to enter inside my tent with a knife to kill us, we can defend ourselves."

17-year-old Yazidi survivor, Northern Iraq, 2018

Our Objectives

What We Set Out to Do

  • Provide evidence-based, trauma-informed self-defense and personal safety instruction to women and girls in communities affected by conflict, displacement, or violence.
  • Identify, train, and certify local women as leaders and coaches so that capability persists independent of ongoing foreign support.
  • Build sustainable local leadership networks within each community we serve.
